## via.5a / m2.5: fixed repo-side

Cause: every via1 landing in the repo-drawn `ROUTE` cells sat on a 0.30 um wire or pad, i.e. 0.075 um of metal per
side, and the 0.8.0 rule wants >= 0.085 um on two adjacent sides. All of these via sites are drawn by this repo's
sub-block scripts, not by `klt gen`. Change: a 0.32 um (0.15 + 2 x 0.085) met1 and met2 pad at each via1 site.

- `layout/bin/_geometry_common.py` (`VIA1_SITE_PAD_UM`, `step_down_to_met1`'s final met1 pad)
- `layout/sampling-frontend/bin/build_layout.py` (same constant and final pad; track vias)
- `layout/halflsb-offset/bin/build_layout.py` (track vias)
- `layout/comparator/bin/build_layout.py` (`Router.add_via`)

Per sub-block `route.gds` under 0.8.0 DRC: comparator 88 -> 0, halflsb-offset 46 -> 0, sampling-frontend 114 -> 0
(counts are the two families together; the 0.8.0 totals on the old route cells were 53+35, 30+16, 80+34).
The old stream also attributed 24 of these markers to `diff_pair` / `cap_array` cells (source-cell names in the

## poly.8 and via3.width.1: artefacts of the 0.6.0 generators, no repo-side fix

Evidence that the geometry comes from generator output, not from this repo's code:

- No repo script draws poly/diffusion for these (the `ROUTE` cells contain no 65/20 or 66/20 shapes; the
  `cdac_array` poly comes from `klt gen mos_array` units).
- `klt gen mos_array` (nfet w=1.0 and pfet w=2.0, l=0.15, the cdac units) generated by klt 0.6.0 and checked with
  0.8.0: `poly.endcap.diff.1` x1 each. Generated by klt 0.8.0: clean.
- `klt gen cap_array {"num": 1}` generated by 0.6.0 and checked with 0.8.0: `via3.width.1` x1; generated by 0.8.0: clean.
- All 6 comparator, 7 halflsb-offset and 22 sampling-frontend blocks, regenerated with klt 0.8.0 through each
  sub-block's own `gen_blocks.py` into `/tmp` and checked with 0.8.0 DRC: every block clean.

So both families disappear when the cells are regenerated by 0.8.0 (the closed klayout-tools#3413 / #2936 work), which
is the pin bump (#734), out of scope here. Not measured: the composed layouts built from 0.8.0-generated cells
(cell sizes change with the endcap, so placement/route checks, LVS and the full top-level 0.8.0 DRC must be re-run in #734).
Not tested: whether 0.8.0 draws `cap_array`'s 0.22 um via3 inside `areaid_mt`; the 0.8.0 output simply has 0.20 um via3.

No new `klayout-tools` issue filed: the generator side already shipped in the closed tickets above, and there is
no remaining tool gap shown by these measurements. No spec line touched, no deck edit, no waiver.
