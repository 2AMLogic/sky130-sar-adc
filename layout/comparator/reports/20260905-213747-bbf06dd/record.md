## AC4: is the routing-driven component material against device mismatch?

**Input-referred parasitic-driven offset estimate: +0.52118 mV** (Vindiff=0 pick-off differential on the extracted netlist, converted through the extracted-side's own gain).

Compared against the device-mismatch-only offset `sim/comparator-decision/records/20260821-071918-433a294.md` reports (`tt_mm`, N=16, seed=1): mean **35.24 mV**, stdev **97.08 mV**.

- 0.52118 mV is 1.479% of the mismatch record's mean and 0.537% of its stdev (i.e. the mean is 68x larger, the stdev 186x larger, than this estimate).
- **Conclusion: the routing-driven component is noise against the device-mismatch term, not material at this block's offset budget.** The parasitic-driven offset estimate above is over an order of magnitude smaller than either the mean or the stdev of the device-mismatch-only offset distribution -- device mismatch, not routing/parasitic imbalance, is what would need to shrink to move this comparator's offset budget. No floorplan change is warranted on offset grounds; the router should not be re-litigated for this reason (per the wire-area record's own note).
- This conclusion is about **offset** specifically. It does not re-open regeneration-time/speed symmetry (a second-order, non-offset contributor `layout/comparator/README.md` already scopes out of this issue's acceptance criteria) or noise.
