## 3. Attribution: what drives the spread

**(a) The PDK mismatch model, quantified.** The sky130 MiM Pelgrom figure for the drawn 1.9000 um plate (DR-019) is `sigma(C_u)/C_u = 1.4737 %` (`A_C = 2.8 %*um`). The measured per-transition random sigmas follow the switched-unit-count combinatorial shape (Pearson r = 0.859 DNL, 0.955 INL): transitions that switch more unit caps really are noisier, in the McCreary-Wooley proportion. The implied sigma_u depends on the unit convention (the source campaign reports ADC-LSB units on a differential structure, DR-003's analytic figure was single-ended array-LSB):

| fit | convention | implied sigma_u |
|---|---|---|
| DNL | single-ended, array-LSB | 2.7542 % |
| DNL | differential, array-LSB | 1.9475 % |
| DNL | differential, ADC-LSB | 0.9737 % |
| INL (amplitude) | single-ended, array-LSB | 3.2798 % |
| INL (amplitude) | differential, array-LSB | 2.3192 % |
| INL (amplitude) | differential, ADC-LSB | 1.1596 % |

Under the like-for-like differential ADC-LSB convention the draws imply 0.9737 % (DNL) / 1.1596 % (INL) against the model's 1.4737 % -- same order, convention-limited agreement.

**(b) FET switches exonerated (caps-only probe).** Rebuilding the seed-1 campaign netlist with every nfet/pfet replaced by an ideal drive (bit=0: 1 ohm tie to VREFN; bit=1: PWL source stepping to VREFP at 2.1n, after the reset switch opens) leaves ONLY the caps' AGAUSS mismatch, and reproduces the committed campaign's per-code level spread in pattern AND magnitude (probe logs committed under `probe-caps-only-20261121-075648-75b91e85`):

The mid-split codes carry 1.0-1.7 mV of per-draw level jitter in BOTH; the endpoints are quiet in BOTH. The spread is unit-cap mismatch, not switch mismatch.

**(c) Structural finding: the harness measures cross-copy, not within-die, DNL.** The campaign fragment (correctly, for a transfer-curve experiment) gives every code its OWN array copy -- so a per-draw 'DNL' between adjacent codes mixes the two copies' independent level errors. On a single die the static bits cancel in a code-to-code difference; across copies they do not. Evidence, all from the committed draws:

- Cross-code level randoms are mutually uncorrelated (Pearson r between -0.42 and +0.23 over the noisiest code pairs) -- independent copies, not shared per-bit errors.
- The 1-unit transitions (0,1)/(2,3)/(510,511) are quiet (sigma 0.010-0.016 LSB) while (384,385) -- also a 1-unit transition -- carries sigma 0.375 LSB and holds the draw maximum in 6/40 draws: its endpoint copies (bits {7,8} and {0..6}) carry large independent level variance that a within-die DNL would cancel.
- Consequence: the campaign's max|DNL| statistic is a CONSERVATIVE structure on the within-die DNL a real die would show (it adds copy-to-copy variance on top). This study sizes the closure against the campaign's own metric -- closing THAT also closes within-die DNL. Changing the methodology (a shared-array testbench) is OUT OF SCOPE here per the issue's bounds; recorded as a question for a separate follow-up, not acted on.

**(d) The systematic floor is mismatch-free and switched-block-scaled.** With mismatch disabled the maxima are 0.2364 (DNL) / 0.1534 (INL) LSB, deterministic across seeds. Per transition the systematic DNL is negative and grows with the switched block: -0.0009 LSB at the 1-unit transitions through -0.0459 at (31,32)'s 63 units, -0.1028 at (63,64)'s 127, -0.1653 at the 255-unit carries (127,128)/(383,384), -0.2364 at the 511-unit MSB carry (255,256) -- see table 1. Area does not remove this term (it is present with mismatch off); the closure model holds it fixed, which is the conservative choice.

## 4. What the 22-code set can and cannot attribute

- CAN: every major-carry pair of the 9-bit sub-array (13 adjacent transitions including the textbook-worst MSB carry 255->256), the endpoints the INL line fit uses, and a short code-density run near 0.
- CANNOT: the other ~499 transitions of a full 512-code ramp; any code-ordered systematic vanishing at the sampled codes; the 10th-bit differential-sign structure (array-only scope, source record's UNITS note); WITHIN-DIE DNL isolation (see 3c).
- The PDK's MiM mismatch is per-instance random AGAUSS with no spatial-correlation term: the draws model ZERO die-gradient, so no layout-ordering effect can be attributed (or excluded) by this campaign -- option C's evidentiary gap, below.
