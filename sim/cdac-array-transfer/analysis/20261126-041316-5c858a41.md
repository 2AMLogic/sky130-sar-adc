## 3. Attribution: what drives the spread

**(a) The PDK mismatch model, quantified.** The sky130 MiM Pelgrom figure for the drawn 1.9000 um plate (DR-019) is `sigma(C_u)/C_u = 1.4737 %` (`A_C = 2.8 %*um`). The sampled DNL sigmas correlate with the square root of the within-array switched-unit count (r = 0.859). The INL sigmas correlate with sqrt(code*(511-code)/511) (r = 0.955). A one-side unit step is 1.8 V / 512 = 3.515625 mV, one ADC LSB. Independent P and N errors add in quadrature, giving a differential coefficient of 1.4142 ADC LSB. Fitting these shapes to the campaign gives effective unit mismatch of 1.9475 % (DNL) and 2.3192 % (INL). The cross-copy experiment limits interpretation of these fits.

**(b) Caps-only isolation probe.** Rebuilding the seed-1 campaign netlist with every nfet/pfet replaced by an ideal drive (bit=0: 1 ohm tie to VREFN; bit=1: PWL source stepping to VREFP at 2.1n, after the reset switch opens) leaves ONLY the caps' AGAUSS mismatch, and reproduces the committed campaign's per-code level spread in pattern AND magnitude (probe logs committed under `probe-caps-only-20261121-075648-75b91e85`):

| 1 | 22.9 | 13.1 |
| 127 | 1171.5 | 1376.3 |
| 128 | 1405.0 | 1621.6 |

The mid-split codes carry 1.0-1.7 mV of level jitter in both experiments and the endpoints are quiet in both. Unit-cap mismatch reproduces the observed pattern and magnitude.

**(c) The harness measures cross-copy DNL.** Each code uses an independent array copy. On a shared array, the variance of the step includes the covariance between its two levels. That covariance can have either sign.

- The 1-unit transitions (0,1)/(2,3)/(510,511) are quiet (sigma 0.010-0.016 LSB) while (384,385) -- also a 1-unit transition -- carries sigma 0.375 LSB and holds the draw maximum in 6/40 draws. Its independent copies give substantial level error even for the unchanged bits.
- At the 255->256 carry the selected sets are disjoint. For ideal independent units, the one-side normalized sensitivity is g(c,i) = selected(c,i) - c/512. With independent copies, sum(g255² + g256²) = 255*(1-255/512) + 256*(1-256/512) = 255.998. On a shared array sum((g256 - g255)²) = 510.998 because sum(g255*g256) = -255*256/512. Thus the cross-copy variance can also be smaller. Per the ruling in #1034, only the campaign metric is used for sizing. Within-die DNL remains unmeasured.

**(d) Mismatch-disabled controls.** The source maxima (absolute DNL / INL) are 0.236446 / 0.153445 LSB, and the pilot maxima are 0.122668 / 0.085145 LSB. Each control is deterministic across its seeds. The model holds the source values fixed when scaling the random terms. The two campaigns also used different PDK and ngspice versions, so the difference between their controls cannot be attributed to plate size alone.

## 4. What the 22-code set can and cannot attribute

- CAN: every major-carry pair of the 9-bit sub-array (13 adjacent transitions including the textbook-worst MSB carry 255->256), the endpoints the INL line fit uses, and a short code-density run near 0.
- CANNOT: the other ~499 transitions of a full 512-code ramp; any code-ordered systematic vanishing at the sampled codes; the 10th-bit differential-sign structure (array-only scope, source record's UNITS note); WITHIN-DIE DNL isolation (see 3c).
- The PDK's MiM mismatch is per-instance random AGAUSS with no spatial-correlation term: the draws model ZERO die-gradient, so no layout-ordering effect can be attributed (or excluded) by this campaign -- option C's evidentiary gap, below.

## 5. Closure options against `<= +-1 LSB` at target_yield 0.99

The fitted upper bound is mean + 2.326*sigma. Margin = (limit - bound)/limit with a limit of 1 LSB. A nonnegative margin meets the fitted criterion. "emp" is the pass fraction of the N=40 rescaled draws. Pelgrom scaling: random parts x 1.9/p, systematic fixed.

### Option A -- larger unit capacitor (pure Pelgrom scaling). RECOMMENDED (see DR-027).

- Minimum plate meeting both normal margins on the 5 nm grid: **3.100 um** (unit-cap area x2.66). The candidate **3.300 um** adds ~5% headroom below the limit on the binding (DNL) statistic.
- The all-40-draws-pass bound is 4.320 um (unit area x5.17) -- dominated by the single worst draw (seed 7, whose (255,256) random part of -1.735 LSB is a 3-4 sigma tail sample of the copy-level distribution). Sizing to one N=40 worst draw with zero headroom is exactly the anti-pattern DR-007's own text rejects; the normal-margin criterion with stated headroom is the sizing basis, and the N>=368 sized run is the empirical resolver.
- Layout cost (from `layout/cdac-array/bin/cdac_layout.py` constants, no re-layout done): unit pitch 3.400 -> 4.800 um assuming the existing 1.500 um gap. Estimated array core 217.6 x 54.4 -> 307.2 x 76.8 um (x1.99 area). The proposed pitch lies on the 5 nm grid. Layout precheck and spacing verification require a candidate layout.
- Ratified-row impact: reopens the RATIFIED Sampling-cap row (C_u, plate side) -- DR-027 proposes the change for operator approval. DR-003's ~415x capacitance margin over the thermal floor grows by 2.8033x (24.288/8.664 fF). RMS thermal noise falls by a factor of 1.6743 at fixed temperature. Issue #1033 measures the settling and acquisition impact at the larger C. Incidental: DR-022/025's systematic gain error scales down with C_par/C_u (fixed parasitics over a larger unit), reported there, not claimed here.

### Option B -- unary segmentation of the top t bits at constant total C

As a sensitivity study, scale every measured random DNL term by the square root of the worst within-array switched-unit count divided by the binary maximum (511 units per side). The count is enumerated over all 511 adjacent pairs. With one thermometer MSB the array is still binary. Independent copies also contribute level errors from unchanged bits, so this uniform carry scaling cannot predict segmented cross-copy yield. Both B and D use this assumption.

### Option D -- segmentation + the INL-sized plate

- t=2: plate 2.335 um (unit area x1.51), DNL emp 0.975 (margin +0.047), INL emp 0.975 (margin +0.000).
- t=3: plate 2.335 um (unit area x1.51), DNL emp 1.000 (margin +0.257), INL emp 0.975 (margin +0.000).

At the model sizes D uses less unit-cap area than A (x1.51 vs x3.02). It reopens the Sampling-cap row (C_u still moves), adds a thermometer decoder to the SAR sequencer (re-composition of the digital partition, DR-006 timing re-verification), splits the MSB routing into 2^t - 1 unary groups, and widens the re-verification surface to every campaign that exercises the sequencer. Kept as the documented area-optimized alternative; not recommended first (see DR-027's reasoning).

### Option C -- layout matching changes (common centroid, dummy ring, routing symmetry)

- NOT evidencable by this campaign's methodology: the PDK mismatch model in the committed draws is per-instance random with no spatial term, so no layout-ordering change can move a simulated number here.
- Both decks show systematic error with mismatch disabled. Neither campaign measures the effect of changing layout order or routing on this error.
- Retained as good practice for the FOLLOW-UP layout (hedging a real-die gradient the model cannot see), carrying no predicted-yield number in this study.

All 16 pilot draws clear +-1 LSB on both statistics; the measured means/sigmas land on the model's prediction (DNL within 0.01 LSB of the predicted mean; INL measured slightly better than predicted). The klt yield envelope (record 20261126-034358-5a2e0198) reports empirical 1.000 with normal-fit cpk 0.93 (DNL) / 1.79 (INL), a required N of 368 to CLAIM 0.99 at this pass rate, and its declared negative control (the as-built 1.9000 um campaign's committed draws) is not yet statistically distinguishable from the nominal at N=16 -- both are the pilot's stated size limits, resolved by the sized run.
