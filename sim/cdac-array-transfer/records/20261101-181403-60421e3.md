The analytic figure is NOT supported by this measurement at the sampled corner: the measured 3-sigma is 0.020x the analytic value (51x smaller). Resolution caveat: ngspice prints each `.meas` level to 6 significant digits, so the spans are quantized at 10 uV = 0.0028 LSB; the measured stdev is only ~3 quantization steps, so its last digits are resolution-limited, which does not affect the ~50x gap to the analytic figure. This record does not establish why the two differ and does not claim the analytic figure is wrong for any other definition of gain error; it only reports what this span-referred metric measured.
