## Interpretation (checked against the cited outputs)

1. **PDK pin effect on this bench: zero at the logged precision.** Local new−old is 0.0e+00 on all ten measurements; the model diff shows the only `ff`-closure differences are a content-preserving file split and additive params no bench device references. A PDK contribution to the archived 3e-05 V delta is therefore not supported by anything measured or diffed here.
2. **The archived baseline is reproducible.** Both local legs (a different host and ngspice 47 vs the baseline's ngspice 46) reproduce the baseline's ten `ff` values exactly. The baseline's own host and binary hash were never recorded — that provenance stays unknown — but its numbers are not fragile across the factors varied here.
3. **The batch leg is the sole outlier**, low by 1–3e-05 V with a consistent sign on every differing pair. The factors uncontrolled between the batch leg and all three agreeing legs are its **ngspice build** (binary `dc859418...`, an ngspice-46 built 2026-10-01; the baseline's binary hash is unknown, so "both ngspice-46" never established same-binary) and its **execution host**. These four legs cannot separate build from host; that comparison (same deck on the exact `dc859418` binary on a controlled host, or a batch rerun) is outside this issue's two-run scope.
4. Solver-internal observation, recorded not interpreted beyond the data: the ngspice-46 baseline `ff_27c_1.80v` log carries 330 `Reference value :` lines (0 -> 5.00000e-07) while each ngspice-47 log prints 7; between the two local pins those 7 values differ slightly (first non-zero `4.72141e-08` old vs `4.82141e-08` new) even though all ten measurements are identical — i.e. the pin bump perturbs something internal to the transient integration without moving the settled measurements. Neither local log carries any warning or error.

## Consequence for #811

The Step 1 discrepancy is **not** a PDK-bump effect: `c6d73a3` and `f6eeac7` are numerically interchangeable for this bench (measured: bit-identical at the logged precision; model diff: no changed parameter for the bench's device set), and the baseline is reproducible on a third host/binary. What remains is a batch-environment effect (runner ngspice build and/or host), sign-consistent at ~1e-5 V scale on one corner. Generic tool gap filed: 2AMLogic/klayout-tools#6973 (no numeric-reproducibility contract across runner builds/hosts; no client-side binary pin/verify). Whether the pilot-local 2e-5 V rule tolerates that class of variation is #811's decision to make with this evidence; no tolerance, spec line, or park state is changed here.
