# VINP/VINN input-drive-impedance budget -- full PVT grid -- 20261113-110421-e03343e

- **Record ID**: 20261113-110421-e03343e
- **Claim**: none against any spec row -- characterization. First measurement in this repo of how much series source resistance the `VINP`/`VINN` pads tolerate while the sampling front end still acquires a new full-scale differential input inside the acquisition window. Reported informationally against the provisional differential LSB as a reference scale, at two tiers, on the ratified 9-point PVT grid. No spec line is changed and no decision record is proposed.
- **Issue**: #847
- **Method**: the unmodified sampling front-end fragment (`sim/sampling-frontend/testbench/sampling_frontend_dut.spice`, read in place) with one ideal source in series with a swept `R_source` on EACH of `VINP` and `VINN` (both resistors always the same value). Stimulus and reading convention are `sim/sampling-acquisition-settling/run_acquisition_settling.py`'s two-pulse old-value-to-new-value full-scale step and its FIXED-TIME `find v(node) at=<t>` probe (no crossing search -- see that runner's own two documented `.meas TRIG/TARG` pitfalls). The probe is taken exactly one acquisition window after SAMPLE pulse #2's 50 % rising edge (160.1 ns). Budgets follow `sim/vcm-drive-budget/`'s `find_budget()`: the largest swept resistance still within the tier.
- **Swept `R_source`**: 0, 100, 300, 1000, 3000, 10000, 30000, 100000 ohm (`0` is emitted as 1e-06 ohm -- see the runner's `R_IDEAL_OHM`). Both acquisition windows are covered: `worst` = DR-006 worst-case (12 MHz, 83.333 ns); `legacy` = legacy (400.0 ns) testbench convention.
- **Control row**: every corner and window carries its own `R_source = 0` row, and every budget below is computed on the error RELATIVE to that row. The raw error against the ideal target (1.4 V differential) is tabulated as well. This matters because the zero-resistance baseline is not zero everywhere: `sim/sampling-acquisition-settling/records/20260908-051436-6ccd72d.md` reports a -0.3800 mV per-node residual at `tt_27c_1.62v` with an ideal source (~0.11 provisional LSB), so a 0.1-LSB tier read against the ideal target could fail there with no source resistance at all.
- **Corner matrix run**: process=['ff', 'fs', 'sf', 'ss', 'tt'], temperature_c=[-40.0, 27.0, 125.0], supply_v=[1.62, 1.8, 1.98] (9 points, one-at-a-time per sim/README.md)
- **Overall**: COMPLETE (9/9 corners complete at both windows)

## What this testbench does not model

- **Series resistance only.** The testbench puts one ideal source in series with one resistor on each input pin. It models no pad capacitance, no ESD network, no anti-alias capacitor at the pad node and no package or bond-wire inductance. A real pad network with shunt capacitance at the die side can behave better (it holds charge locally) or worse (it rings with inductance) than this; neither is measured here.
- **A full-scale step between consecutive samples is the stimulus.** `VINP`/`VINN` step the whole rail-adjacent differential span (0.2 V <-> 1.6 V per side) during HOLD, so every figure below is the worst case for a near-Nyquist input. A slowly varying input presents a far smaller step and is not graded.
- **`Csamp` stands in for the CDAC array.** The DUT is the sampling front-end fragment, whose per-side `Csamp` is sized (DR-004) to mimic the real array's load. The whole-ADC CDAC, its own parasitics and the comparator input are not in this deck; `sim/top-plate-cpar/` measures the top-plate parasitic separately and it is not added here.
- **`VCM`, the references and the supply are ideal.** `VCM` is a fixed ideal 0.9 V source -- `sim/sampling-acquisition-settling/`'s own convention, carried over unchanged so this campaign's control row is comparable with that campaign's records; it does NOT track the supply corner. `VREFP`/`VREFN` are absent from this fragment, and the supply is an ideal source at the corner's own voltage. `VCM`'s own drive impedance is `sim/vcm-drive-budget/`'s campaign, the references' is #841's, and the supply terminals' is `sim/supply-impedance-sensitivity/`'s; none is combined with this one.
- **The SAMPLE level tracks the supply; the input levels do not.** One deck serves all nine corners, so SAMPLE is a normalised `pwl` scaled by `v(VDD)` through a behavioural source, while the 0.2/1.6 V input extremes are fixed at every corner -- exactly as in the mirrored acquisition-settling runner. At 1.62 V the input extremes are therefore a slightly larger fraction of the supply than at 1.98 V.
- **The budget's resolution is the sweep list.** A budget is the largest SWEPT resistance that still passes, so it is a lower bound on the true continuous threshold, quantised to this log-spaced list (0, 100, 300, 1000, 3000, 10000, 30000, 100000 ohm). A budget reported as `>= 100000` is right-censored by the list's top value, not measured.
- **No spec row is graded.** `spec/target-spec.md` is entirely DRAFT (#1/#27). The provisional differential LSB (3.5156 mV, DR-003 Item 2 pending #27) is a reference scale only, and the DR-006 window is downstream of the DRAFT sample-rate row. `spec/target-spec.md`'s DRAFT Kickback row names a 1 kOhm series source impedance on these pins and is the reason 1 kOhm is a simulated point here; this record does not grade that row and changes no spec line.
