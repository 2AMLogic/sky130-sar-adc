## Result -- verdict on the DRAFT sample-rate row at the candidate plate

**Verdict criterion (a reference scale, not a ratified gate).** An endpoint of the DRAFT `100 kS/s--1 MS/s` row is reported as HOLDING iff (a) the worst grid corner's DIFFERENTIAL acquisition error at that deadline stays within the provisional differential half-LSB (1.7578 mV, DR-003 Item 2 pending #27), and (b) the bit-trial settling at that deadline leaves the phase with more time than the comparator decision (4.3575 ns) plus sequencer logic (0.4237 ns) delays need (their own PVT-complete binding figures, cited in the sample-rate entry of `sim/report/manifest.py`). ## What this record does not establish

- Both benches are sub-block settling measurements at an EXPERIMENTAL plate size: passing or failing them does not by itself set the sample rate, and whole-ADC code correctness (the #263/#259 line of work) remains open independently.
- The front end here is the shipping front end driving ~2.8x the array capacitance with UNCHANGED sampling switches, exactly the configuration whose shortfall #1033 asks quantified. The study does not resize anything; a resize needs its own DR-011/DR-014 charge-injection/kickback evidence, and any target-table change goes through a decision record (DR-027's ratification path), never an edit.
- As in the baseline grid: one step direction of the worst-case differential stimulus, the previous-code state fixed at every grid point (with the control table above), and the held-bit parasitic omission inherited from the companion bench's own documented reduction.
