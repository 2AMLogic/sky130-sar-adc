The 99 % threshold is a fraction of each row's OWN step (ratio x supply), not a fixed LSB accuracy -- a small-swing row whose converged value differs from the closed-form ideal by more than 1 % of that step never crosses however long the window, which is exactly why the phase-end (`pend_*`) and late (`plate`) error columns above are reported alongside: they separate a still-settling node (phase-end error >> late error) from a settled offset (both similar, and time-invariant).

## What this record does not establish

- It measures ONE mechanism (the array's own switch-Ron settling) at an EXPERIMENTAL plate size. Passing or failing it does not by itself set the sample rate: the sampling front end's acquisition at the same assembled load is the companion record's own question (and, at this plate size, the binding one), and whole-ADC code correctness remains open independently.
- The held-bit ideal-source simplification (and its omitted held-bit switch parasitics) is inherited unchanged from the baseline campaign's documented reduction, as is the assumption that the SEL edge starts the phase (DR-006's one-CLK-per-phase allocation, with the sequencer's own CLK-to-phase delay quoted separately above rather than subtracted from the budget).
- Nothing here ratifies DR-027, edits a target-table row, or resizes a switch: if the rate verdict needs drive strength moved, that is a separate design change with its own DR-011/DR-014 charge-injection/kickback evidence (#1033's scope rule).
