## Method (unchanged from the campaign)

The reduced sub-model, band and conversion of `sim/comparator-decision/comparator_campaigns/noise.py` (issue #28; DR-004 Amendment A via #175), transcribed device-for-device into `sim/comparator-decision/testbench/klt/tb_body.spice`: tail + input pair with the DI-node precharge PMOS pair diode-connected as the loads on DIP/DIN, cross-coupled latch pairs omitted (off until regeneration), CLK held at VDD, noise taken at `v(dip,din)`, AC stimulus single-ended on `Vinp` (AC 1). Band 1 kHz - 1 GHz (`NOISE_FSTART_HZ`/`NOISE_FSTOP_HZ`), `dec 20` sweep; ngspice's input-referred noise is therefore single-ended and the differential figure is `sqrt(2) x` it (the diff-pair noise-doubling identity, applied as the named, flagged approximation DR-003 Item 4 records). The request's `options.ngspice_init` selects `option sparse` (KLU does not implement `.noise`; the same runtime switch noise.py makes over the repo-wide `option klu`) plus the repo spiceinit's remaining settings, so the run does not depend on the worker's `$HOME/.spiceinit`. In the envelope, `inoise_se_rms` is `klt sim`'s `noise` measurement (`quantity: inoise`, `band_hz: [1e3, 1e9]`, trapezoidal on the swept grid) and `inoise_diff_rms` is the `expr` `inoise_se_rms * sqrt(2)` carrying `limits: {max: 1.0148e-3}` and `spec_row: comparator_noise`.
